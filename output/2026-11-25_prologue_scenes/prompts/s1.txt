The Throne. Hell's vast throne hall, symmetrical and majestic. Baal sits on a towering black stone throne at the top of a high stepped dais, seen from the foot of the steps; his face is lost in shadow under the horned crown. Seven hooded servants stand in a row on the steps below him, faces hidden under their hoods, each hood a different deep muted color. Rivers of lava glow behind the throne, smoke under a dark vaulted ceiling.
