The Fall. Baal's whole body falls backward down an endless dark chasm, seen from the side at medium distance so the full figure reads clearly: head and back toward the bottom, both arms flung up, legs trailing above, his torn crimson cloak streaming upward. His crown is gone and his face is turned away into shadow. Far above him the red light of Hell shrinks to a narrow glowing rift in the rock. A glowing brand mark burns into the skin of his bare back. Correct, readable anatomy - one head, two arms, two legs.
