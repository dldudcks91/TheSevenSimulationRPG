# Chapter 2 stage 2 Ent portraits

The three individual PNGs in this folder are rejected first drafts. They were generated without the project's required appearance and style references, shared sheet, black eyes, and portrait proportions.

The revised comparison sheet is `src/assets/art/faces/source/_scratch/ch2_ent_2x2_portrait_draft_v4.png`. It uses the stage 202 trees for appearance, role-specific existing portraits for gear, and monster 1401 for linework and proportions. The upper left, upper right, and lower left cells are the knight, archer, and mage; the lower right cell is a spare variant.

This is a selection draft. No monster data or in-game portrait files have been changed.

The latest comparison candidate is `src/assets/art/faces/source/_scratch/ch2_ent_3x3_minimal_prompt_v1_green_grid.png`. Its exact short generation prompt and ordered references are in `minimal_prompt_v1.txt`. Each row is swamp ent knight / vine ent archer / poison-spore ent mage. Its source-alpha sheet and 44px-per-cell check are beside it in `_scratch/`. The previous `hero_monster_style_v1` sheet was used as an appearance reference, so the new sheet intentionally retains much of its morphology. It has not been selected for the game.

The earlier `ingame_goblin_skeleton_v2` sheet was rejected by the user for a mismatched art style. See `style_analysis.md` for the diagnosis. The `ingame_goblin_skeleton_v1` and older `game_style` sheets are also rejected drafts.

The stage 202 background was used only for wood and vine anatomy. The style references for the current sheet were the actual in-game 256px monster WebPs `1101`, `1102`, `1301`, and `1303` (goblins and skeletons). Their ready PNG sources and the other goblin/skeleton in-game portraits were also visually checked. The earlier exploration and cohesive sheets remain archived in `_scratch/`. These are selection drafts; no game data or ready portraits have been changed.
