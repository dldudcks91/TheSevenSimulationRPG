Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: STUN indicator star sprites for The Seven Simulation RPG, a grim dark fantasy party RPG about the Seven Deadly Sins and Satan's armies (Diablo 2 mood). In game, three small stars slide along a black line above a stunned unit's head on a tiny 101px portrait card. Code moves and flips them; you only draw single stars.
Draw in EXACTLY the existing DARK FANTASY CARTOON style shown in reference image 1. The reference is a STYLE sample only; do not copy its 3x3 layout or subjects. Preserve the thick uneven near-black contour, broad angular cel-shaded shapes, two decisive color planes, small crisp highlight and serious, menacing mood. No painterly texture, watercolor, foil, pixel art, 3D render, glossy finish, neon, glow, bloom, sparkle dust or lens flare.
NOT CUTE: the previous attempt was rejected as too cute. No fat rounded proportions, no soft blunt or rounded tips, no toy or sticker look, no bright candy gold or lemon yellow, no comic twinkle stars, no faces or eyes. Every star must look old, crude, harsh and dangerous, as if forged, chipped or broken centuries ago. Sharp angular points of UNEQUAL length, asymmetric silhouette. Points must still be thick enough to read when the star is shrunk to 16px: a few LARGE shapes, never hair-thin needles or ornament.
FLAT FILLS ONLY: every shape is one flat color plus one flat shadow shape. Wear and damage are shown only as a FEW LARGE shapes (for example two big chipped notches or one big crack line), never as grain, noise, speckles, scratches or gradients.
Colors are MID VALUE, never pastel and never near-black, so they read on a dark card. Never magenta, pink or purple.
LAYOUT: ONE SQUARE image containing EXACTLY TWO columns and TWO rows, 4 invisible square cells, ideally 1254x1254. Genuinely transparent alpha background. NO grid lines, cell frames, text, numbering, labels or checkerboard. Each star sits centered in its cell, facing the viewer flat-on, with AT LEAST 15% clear transparent margin on ALL sides. Stars never touch neighbors.
Cells left-to-right, top-to-bottom:
Row 1, column 1: a crude RUSTED IRON five-pointed star with long sharp points of unequal length, one point bent sideways, TWO big chipped notches cut out of its edges. Iron gray body with one large rust-orange shadow plane.
Row 1, column 2: a heavy FOUR-POINTED IRON SPIKE star like a forged caltrop or nail cross: four thick tapering spikes, the vertical pair longer than the horizontal pair, a square rivet-like center. Dull steel with one dark rust shadow plane.
Row 2, column 1: a jagged TARNISHED BRASS star with SIX sharp rays of clearly unequal length, angular and asymmetric like a shard burst. Dull dark ochre body with one darker brown-bronze shadow plane, small pale highlight.
Row 2, column 2: a blackened IRON throwing-star-like five-point star with hooked, slightly curved blade points all bending the same way (spinning look), and ONE large dried dark-red blood stain plane covering two of the points. Charcoal-gray steel with a lighter steel highlight edge.
